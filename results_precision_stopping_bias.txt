==========================================================================================
EXPERIMENT A: PRECISION STOPPING BIAS VALIDATION - WIDTH SWEEP
==========================================================================================

ESTIMAND (Target of Inference):
--------------------------------
We estimate the failure rate of a uniform mixture over strata:

    p = (1/4) Σ_{k=1}^4 p_k

where:
- K = 4 strata
- p_k = per-stratum failure rate
- Uniform weights w_k = 1/K (equal-sized evaluation slices)

For this experiment:
- Strata: ['easy', 'medium', 'hard', 'nightmare']
- Per-stratum p: {'easy': 0.0, 'medium': 0.05, 'hard': 0.1, 'nightmare': 0.4}
- True mixture p: 0.1375

CRITICAL: Both naive and stratified estimate the SAME p under uniform mixture.
The difference appears under optional stopping:

UNCONDITIONAL (fixed n):
  E[p̂_n] = p  (both methods unbiased at any fixed n)

CONDITIONAL (at stopping time τ):
  Naive: E[p̂_τ | stopped] may ≠ p (selection-induced bias)
  Stratified: E[p̂_τ | stopped] = p (balance prevents bias)

PRECISION STOPPING RULE (SWEEP):
  τ(w) = min{n ≥ 30 : width(n) ≤ w} ∪ {200}
  Width targets: w ∈ [0.35, 0.38, 0.4, 0.45]
  Purpose: Demonstrate time-uniform peeking tax at small n

HARM METRICS:
  1. Conditional bias: E[p̂_τ - p]
  2. Absolute error: E[|p̂_τ - p|]
  3. MSE: E[(p̂_τ - p)²]
  4. Coverage at stop: P(p ∈ [L_τ, U_τ])
