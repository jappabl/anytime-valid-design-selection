THE RLA BRIDGE: the design boundary on a risk-limiting election audit
============================================================================
NON-CLAIM: nothing here improves SHANGRLA/BRAVO/ALPHA/UI-TS, and no procedure here is
proposed for use in a real election. Public county totals fix POOL PARAMETERS only.
What is tested is this project's pre-observable DESIGN-SELECTION boundary, in a domain
where a sample is a hand-counted ballot. K=6 ballots/block, 150 reps/arm/cell, +-5% tie band.

  best/worst are taken over the arms that actually certify (>=90% of reps); an arm that
  never finishes is not a cheap audit, and UI is excluded on that ground where it is.
  fixed-n column: one-sided binomial at the best arm's own measured power; it buys NO
  anytime validity (no peeking, no early stop, no escalation), so the premium is the
  price of sequential validity and is reported with whatever sign it has.
