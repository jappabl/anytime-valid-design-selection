4. PREMIUM UNITS -- nats bound vs the sample-count claim
========================================================================================
Pathwise guarantee (verified exactly in audit/check_mixture_recursion.py):
   log E_warm(n) >= log E_cold(n) - c   with c = log(1/eps) (joint) or K log(1/eps) (per-stratum),
   uniformly in n AND after the min over the null boundary.
The artifacts additionally assert sample-count consequences ('<= ~150 samples slower',
'measured +9.9 vs bound +9.2 - consistent', 'worst median <= cold + log(1/eps)/V_rr').
Those are NOT implied: a nats cap on log E does not cap the median of a first-crossing time.

  tau=0.15 V_rr=0.0192  joint-inv     median  1056 vs cold   928  -> extra  +128.0 samples =  +2.46 nats in the artifacts' overhead metric;  nats cap = 2.30 (EXCEEDS)

  tau=0.16 V_rr=0.0127  joint-inv     median  1796 vs cold  1540  -> extra  +256.0 samples =  +3.25 nats in the artifacts' overhead metric;  nats cap = 2.30 (EXCEEDS)

  tau=0.17 V_rr=0.0075  joint-inv     median  3070 vs cold  2782  -> extra  +288.0 samples =  +2.15 nats in the artifacts' overhead metric;  nats cap = 2.30 (WITHIN)

  A median-sample gap exceeding the nats cap is NOT a validity failure -- the cap
  bounds log E, not the median crossing time -- but the artifacts present the two as
  the same quantity.
